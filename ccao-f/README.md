# CCAO-F — Claude Certified Associate – Foundations

Study material for Anthropic's foundational Claude certification, aimed at the people the exam is actually for: project managers, business analysts, delivery leads, and other professionals who use Claude at work without writing code.

The exam's one big idea, which every domain guide here comes back to: **it tests judgment, not memorized features.** Almost every question is a workplace scenario with four plausible answers, one of which is a trap that *feels* responsible.

> Independent study material — not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. All practice questions are original, written for this project. None are from the real exam.

## A note on product facts

Claude's features and model names change over time. The study guides deliberately teach durable judgment rules from Anthropic's published guidance rather than version numbers, prices, or limits — those go stale and the exam's scenarios don't depend on them. When in doubt, check [Anthropic's documentation](https://docs.claude.com).
