## What the format means for how you study

Sixty scenario questions in 120 minutes is two minutes per question. The questions are short to read but each carries one deliberately tempting wrong answer, so the time pressure is on *judgment*, not reading. That's why every guide in this repo ends with a trap analysis — recognizing the trap's shape is faster than reasoning from scratch.

720/1000 means you can miss a meaningful number of questions and still pass. Don't burn five minutes on one item; eliminate the clearly-wrong options, pick, flag, move on (see [exam-tips.md](exam-tips.md), tip 4).
